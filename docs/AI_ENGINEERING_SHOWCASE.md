# Planter AI Engineering Showcase Notes

Planter is intentionally positioned as a portfolio-grade AI engineering project, not just a plant tracker.

## Current showcase signals

- **Local-first architecture:** plant data, notes, model settings, and AI conversations persist in-browser before any cloud dependency.
- **Data portability:** users can export/import a JSON backup, which demonstrates privacy-first product thinking and future sync readiness.
- **AI interaction design:** the assistant grounds responses in selected plant context instead of being a generic chatbot.
- **Image-centered workflow:** progress photos are first-class records, enabling future visual analysis and before/after comparison.
- **Model lifecycle UX:** settings include a local model state machine for a future Gemma 4 0.8B runtime.
- **Health scoring:** plant state, reminders, notes, and photo history combine into a transparent care score.

## Future proof points to add

1. Real on-device model runtime integration.
2. Vision pipeline for plant photo comparison and symptom detection.
3. Eval harness for care-advice quality and hallucination checks.
